Impact and benefits

Citizens

Facts already issued (income, caste, 7/12, marks, land, crop) are fetched live under consent — not re-uploaded, not stored in a lake. One application number. Revoke and the next fetch is denied; nothing to purge.

Officers

One file, not three portals. See which issuer answered. Retry a downed department without restarting the citizen journey. Status in officer words: submitted, in progress, needs action, completed.

Departments

Keep their system of record. Plug in via adapter + mapping. A new scheme is configuration — farmer subsidy already demonstrates this, with a citizen skin on the same core.

State / economic

Avoid a fourth silo or a central data lake. An interoperability layer is cheaper to extend than N×N bilateral integrations.

Social / privacy

Sensitive discovery (for example a caste pointer) is consent-gated. Provenance on every field. Tamper-evident audit for RTI / inquiry. DigiLocker is not treated as consent.

Environment

Fewer duplicate physical certificates and counter visits; digital reuse of already-issued records, fetched on demand.
