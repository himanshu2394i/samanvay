Farmer subsidy

Support for eligible farmers in Maharashtra. This service uses land records already held by Revenue, crop records held by Agriculture, and bank details held by DBT, after you consent.

Who can apply
A cultivator with a land parcel in Maharashtra
What is checked
Land parcel (Revenue), crop record (Agriculture), bank account (DBT)

Apply for subsidy Track an application

How this service works

  1. Fill applicant details used on revenue and agriculture records.
  2. Connect the three department accounts this subsidy needs. This is not a document upload list.
  3. Read the consent and agree to share those records for the subsidy check.
  4. Submit, then track status in everyday words.
