Business licence / NOC / व्यावसायिक परवाना

One application for a municipal trade licence with fire, pollution, and land checks. This service collects only the consent you give, then fetches records already held by Municipal, Fire, Pollution, and Revenue. You do not upload documents here.

Apply for licence Track an application

How this service works

  1. Fill the applicant name and date of birth used on municipal and revenue records.
  2. Link the four department accounts this licence needs. This is not a document upload list.
  3. Read the consent in plain language and agree to share those records for the NOC check.
  4. Submit, then track status in everyday words: submitted, in progress, needs action, or completed.
Scheme year
2026–27
Who can apply
Business premises in Maharashtra needing a municipal licence / NOC
What is checked
Property (Municipal), fire NOC (Fire), pollution clearance (Pollution), land record (Revenue)
