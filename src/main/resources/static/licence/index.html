Business licence / NOC

Permission to start a premises in Maharashtra. This service does not ask you to upload the same papers again. It uses records already held by Municipal, Fire, Pollution, and Revenue, after you consent.

Who can apply
A person opening or continuing a business premises in Maharashtra
What is checked
Property (Municipal), fire NOC (Fire), pollution clearance (Pollution), land record (Revenue)

Apply for licence Track an application

How this service works

  1. Fill applicant details used on municipal and revenue records.
  2. Connect the four department accounts this licence needs. This is not a document upload list.
  3. Read the consent and agree to share those records for the licence check.
  4. Submit, then track status in everyday words.
