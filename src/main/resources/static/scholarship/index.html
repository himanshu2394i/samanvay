Post-Matric Scholarship / उत्तर-माध्यमिक शिष्यवृत्ती

Financial assistance for eligible students after Class 10. This service collects only the consent you give, then checks eligibility using records already held by other departments.

Scheme year
2026–27
Who can apply
Students studying after Class 10 in a recognised institution in Maharashtra
What is checked
Income and caste (Revenue), marks (Education), bank account for payment (DBT)
