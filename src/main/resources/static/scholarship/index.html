Post-Matric Scholarship / उत्तर-माध्यमिक शिष्यवृत्ती

Financial assistance for eligible students after Class 10. This service collects only the consent you give, then checks eligibility using records already held by other departments. You do not upload documents here.

Scheme year
2026–27
Who can apply
Students studying after Class 10 in a recognised institution in Maharashtra
What is checked
Income and caste (Revenue), marks (Education), bank account for payment (DBT)

Apply for scholarship Track an application

How this service works

  1. Fill your name and date of birth — the same details as on your school or college record.
  2. Link the three department accounts this scheme needs. This is not a document upload list.
  3. Read the consent in plain language and agree to share those records for eligibility.
  4. Submit, then track status in everyday words: submitted, in progress, needs action, or completed.
